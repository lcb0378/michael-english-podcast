女生: 欢迎来到 Michael 的早听晚读！我是 Cherry。今天是我们的第十三期！第一位主角是一个大块头南瓜，它比一头犀牛还要重；第二位主角，是一罐在古墓里等了三千年的蜂蜜。一位是刚刚诞生的新冠军，一位是甜蜜的古代宝藏。准备好了吗？出发！

男生: Hello, young explorers, and welcome back to Michael's Morning Listening! Today our first guest is a pumpkin so heavy that it weighs more than a rhinoceros. Our second guest waited three thousand years inside a dark tomb — a pot of honey! Big, sweet, and full of surprises. Let's go!

女生: 先来认识第一位主角。它圆滚滚、橙亮亮，体重超过一千公斤，刚刚打破了南瓜的世界纪录。种出它的人，是一位六十五岁的英国爷爷，今年他第一次和自己的双胞胎兄弟分开参赛。听听这位南瓜冠军的故事。

男生: Meet Bagheera, the heaviest pumpkin in the world! It weighs one thousand, two hundred and ninety-five kilograms — as heavy as a small car, and heavier than a white rhinoceros. Around its middle, it measures more than six metres. Its name comes from a storybook: Bagheera, the black panther in The Jungle Book.

Its grower is Ian, a sixty-five-year-old plant grower from England. Ian and his twin brother Stuart spent more than fifty years growing giant pumpkins together. The brothers became champions and even set a world record together. This year, for the first time, Ian entered the contest alone, determined to beat his own record — and he did!

Growing a champion takes hard work. For two months, Ian worked up to six hours a day. On hot days, he gave his plant five hundred and ninety litres of water — enough to fill three bathtubs! At last, workers lifted Bagheera with a forklift onto a giant scale. A Guinness judge observed every step and announced: a new world record!

Now Bagheera sits on a farm, and families bring their cameras to take photos beside it. And when the show ends? This champion will become dinner — for the cows on the farm! From one tiny seed to a pumpkin heavier than a rhinoceros: what a champion!

女生: 从南瓜地，我们钻进一座三千年前的古墓。第二位主角就藏在墓里的陶罐中——蜂蜜！放了几千年，它怎么一点都没有坏呢？秘密一共有三层，听好啦。

男生: Time for something sweet. Long ago in Egypt, a king was buried with pots of honey — food for his journey to the next world. Three thousand years later, explorers opened the tomb and tasted it. Still good! How can food stay fresh for three thousand years?

Secret number one: honey is very, very dry. Bees make it from nectar, the sweet juice inside flowers, fanning their wings like tiny fans to dry it. Germs need water to live, and inside honey they dry out and die.

Secret number two: honey is a little sour, like lemon juice, and germs hate sour food. Secret number three: bees add a special enzyme that makes a natural cleaner, stopping germs from growing. Then the bees seal every cell with wax, like a lid on a tiny jar.

Dry, sour, guarded by a cleaner, and sealed with wax — no wonder honey can wait three thousand years! Keep the lid closed and the water out, and your honey might wait for you, too.

女生: 学知识时间到！今天的俚语甜甜的：a piece of cake。字面意思是“一块蛋糕”，真正的意思可是大不相同。听男主播讲一个小故事，你马上就懂。

男生: Here's today's idiom: "a piece of cake." It means something is very easy — as easy as eating a sweet slice of cake. Listen to little Tom. His teacher said, "Our class will carve a pumpkin for the autumn festival." Tom smiled and said, "No problem. Carving a pumpkin is a piece of cake!" Ten minutes later, his pumpkin had a funny face, and Tom won first prize. But growing a pumpkin like Bagheera? Ian will tell you: that is NOT a piece of cake!

女生: 今天的故事都听完啦？我们认识了比犀牛还重的南瓜冠军，又揭开了蜂蜜三千年不坏的秘密。现在轮到你们动脑筋啦，听听男主播的问题，想好了可以说给爸爸妈妈听。

男生: Let's think about our two stories. First: if you could grow a giant pumpkin, what would you name it, and what would you do with it? Second: honey stayed sweet for three thousand years. If you could save one food for people in the far future, what would you choose? Talk about it with your family!

女生: 好啦，今天的早听晚读就到这里。愿你像伊恩爷爷一样有耐心，像小蜜蜂一样勤劳。种下一颗小小的种子，说不定哪天就长成了冠军。我们明天早上见！

男生: That's all for today, young explorers. Dream big like Ian, work hard like the bees — and see you tomorrow morning!
