女生: 欢迎来到 Michael 的早听晚读！我是 Cherry。今天我们要去两个冰天雪地的地方：一个是能滑五百多米的巨型冰滑梯，另一个是住在北极圈、眼睛会变色的驯鹿。准备好棉袄了吗？出发！

男生: Hello, young explorers, and welcome back to Michael's Morning Listening! Today we visit two frozen worlds: a giant ice slide, and a reindeer whose eyes change color with the seasons. Put on your warm coat. Let's go!

女生: 先去中国最冷的城市之一——哈尔滨。那里有一座用冰雪建成的乐园，里面有一条超级冰滑梯，从高处一滑到底，耳朵边全是风声！它到底有多长，又是怎么建起来的？竖起耳朵听好啦。

男生: In Harbin, a city in the far north of China, winter builds a whole park out of ice and snow. It is the largest ice and snow park in the world, filled with more than a thousand artworks made of ice and snow. Inside it stands a super ice slide, 521 meters long — longer than five football fields!

Workers cut huge blocks of ice from a frozen river. The ice comes in every format: castles, towers, glowing lanterns, and one very long slide. Twenty-four lanes run side by side, so families can race together. The caption under one photo says it all: "The fastest way to feel like flying."

Outside the park, pedestrians walk past ice houses and bridges, all lit up like a dream. Children climb to the top in thick coats, sit on a soft mat, and push off. A fine spray of ice crystals flies up behind them. They laugh all the way down, deaf to the cold wind. At the bottom, the crowd erupts in cheers, and everyone climbs up to go again. With patience, the workers rebuild the park every winter — and every winter the fun comes back with the snow.

女生: 从冰滑梯上下来，我们飞到更北的地方——北极圈。那里的冬天又黑又长，可驯鹿却能在微光中找到食物、躲开狼群。秘密就藏在它们的眼睛里：到了冬天，眼睛会变蓝！

男生: Now fly far to the north, past the Arctic Circle, where winter nights last for weeks. Reindeer live there, and they must find food and spot wolves in very dim light. Their secret is in their eyes.

Behind the retina, the part of the eye that catches light, reindeer have a mirror-like layer called the tapetum lucidum. In summer, this mirror looks golden. But in winter, it turns a vivid blue. Why? In the long darkness, the reindeer's pupils stay wide open, and the pressure inside the eye rises. The tiny fibers in the mirror pack closer together, and it starts to reflect blue light instead of gold.

Blue is the color of Arctic twilight — the soft blue light that fills the sky for hours each day. With blue eyes, reindeer can see much better in that dim light. They can spot a wolf moving against the snow, and find the pale lichen they love, hidden under the snow. Nature makes no discrimination: every animal must find its own way through winter, and the reindeer found a beautiful one.

The reindeer is like an ambassador for all Arctic animals: a small change inside the body can make a big difference. And when summer returns, the mirror turns golden again.

男生: Here's today's idiom: 'on cloud nine.' When you feel so happy that you feel like floating high in the sky, you are on cloud nine! Here's an example: 'After she raced down the giant ice slide, Mia was on cloud nine all the way home.' When were you last on cloud nine?
