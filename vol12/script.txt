女生: 欢迎来到 Michael 的早听晚读！我是 Cherry。今天是我们的第十二期！第一位主角，是两只在柏林过生日的大熊猫双胞胎姐妹，它们的生日蛋糕居然是用冰做的；第二位主角，是一整片森林——科学家发现，树木在地下藏着一张秘密大网。一块冰蛋糕，一片会“说话”的森林，准备好了吗？出发！

男生: Hello, young explorers, and welcome back to Michael's Morning Listening! This is our twelfth show together! Today we fly to a zoo in Berlin, where two birthday girls share one very cold cake. Then we tiptoe into a forest, where the trees are whispering under the ground. Let's go!

女生: 先飞到德国的柏林动物园。有一对大熊猫双胞胎姐妹刚刚过完两岁生日，饲养员为她们准备了一个超特别的蛋糕——三层的、粉粉的，而且是冰做的！她们是怎么过这个生日的呢？听好啦。

男生: Happy birthday, Leni and Lotti! The twin sisters are giant pandas at Berlin Zoo. For their second birthday, their keepers made them a cake — a cake of ice! It had three tiers, pink and orange, coloured with beetroot and carrot juice. On top sat little flowers carved from carrots. No candles — pandas like presents frozen. The sisters wasted no time. They nibbled the carrot flowers, pawed at the ice, and chased each other around the garden. The crowd erupted in cheers; the cameras clicked and clicked. The children at the glass had eyes round as saucers. At the start, the twins were tiny — born blind, almost hairless, small enough to sleep in a keeper's hand. Leni weighed just one hundred and thirty-six grams — about as much as an apple! Today, each sister weighs more than fifty kilos. Their parents, Meng Meng and Jiao Qing, came from Chengdu, China — the second pair of panda twins ever born in Germany. Every panda birthday is a party for conservation: pandas were once in great danger, and each healthy baby shows the hard work is working. Happy birthday, Leni and Lotti — may your cake always be cold!

女生: 吃完冰蛋糕，我们钻进一片安静的森林。地面上，树叶在风里跳舞；地面下，却藏着一张看不见的大网。科学家说，大树会通过这张网互相送吃的、报信儿。这张神奇的网到底是什么呢？听好啦。

男生: Now tiptoe into the forest. Above the ground, birds sing among the leaves; below the ground, something amazing is happening. Almost every tree is plugged into a secret network — not of wires, but of fungi: tiny threads, thinner than hair, wrapping root tips in soft nets, like little capsules of cotton. Through it, trees trade: fungi carry water and minerals up, and trees pay with sugar from their leaves. Nobody gets everything, but everybody gets enough. That is a compromise — a fair deal written in sugar and water. Scientists observed the forest for years to determine what else moves below ground. When insects attack one tree, it sends a warning through the network — a warning relevant to every neighbour: "Make your leaves bitter — the hungry bugs are coming!" Mother trees turn up the dial and send extra sugar to their seedlings. Is the forest really "talking"? Not with words — but signals and supplies move from tree to tree, and the forest works like one big family. It sounds almost like justice: the strong help the weak. Next time you hug a tree, remember: under your feet, the forest is whispering.

女生: 学知识时间到！今天的俚语是 once in a blue moon。蓝月亮是特别罕见的满月，所以这个短语说的就是“难得一次、千载难逢”。听听男主播的小故事，看看这个短语怎么用。

男生: Here's today's idiom: "once in a blue moon." A blue moon is a rare full moon, so this means: very rarely. Listen: my neighbour, Mr. Lee, washes his car once in a blue moon — maybe twice a year. But last Sunday it rained all morning — and Mr. Lee washed his car in the rain! "The rain is helping me," he laughed. And you? What do you do once in a blue moon — once a year, not once a fortnight?

女生: 今天的故事都听完啦？我们先陪熊猫姐妹吃了冰蛋糕，又偷听了森林地下的悄悄话。现在轮到你们动脑筋啦，听听男主播的问题，想好了可以说给爸爸妈妈听。

男生: Let's think. The panda twins shared a frozen cake; the forest shared sugar and secrets. Two questions for you. First: if you made a birthday cake for an animal, which animal, and what flavour? Second: if the trees could send you one message, what would they say? Talk about it with your family!

女生: 好啦，今天的早听晚读就到这里。谢谢你一直陪着我们。愿你像大树一样照顾朋友，像熊猫姐妹一样甜甜蜜蜜。我们明天早上见！

男生: That's all for today, young explorers. Be kind like a mother tree, and sweet like a panda's birthday. See you tomorrow morning!
