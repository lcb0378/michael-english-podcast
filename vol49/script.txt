女生: 先说五位英雄狗狗。在南美洲的厄瓜多尔，五只搜救犬刚刚结束了工作生涯。它们和消防员一起，在地震和山体滑坡后寻找被困的人们。快听听它们的故事。

男生: In Ecuador, five rescue dogs have just retired. Their names are Ares, Kratos, Zeus, Titan and Gaia. For seven years, they worked with firefighters, looking for people after earthquakes and landslides. They were German shepherds and golden retrievers, and their approach was simple: follow the nose, never give up.

It was not easy. They climbed over broken stones and searched through dust, rain and heat. More than once, a dog found a person under the stones — and the rescuers could feel the person's pulse. He was alive! The dogs did not just save lives; they gave families hope again.

Now the five heroes are off duty. At a ceremony, the firefighters said thank you, and each dog went home with a new family. Gaia's new owner, Valeria, said she could not believe her new pet used to save lives. Now Gaia's job is easier: long walks, good food, and a soft bed. After years of hard work, these heroes have earned their rest.

女生: 从英雄狗狗的退役生活，我们来看看狗狗鼻子里的秘密：为什么它的嗅觉比我们强几万倍？答案可能会让你大吃一惊，快听！

男生: So how does a dog's nose do such amazing work? Here is one amazing aspect: a dog's sense of smell is 10,000 to 100,000 times more sensitive than ours. Inside a dog's nose there are up to 300 million smell receptors — tiny scent sensors. We humans have only about six million. And the part of a dog's brain that reads smells is about 40 times bigger than ours.

How does it work? When a dog sniffs, air goes into two rooms inside its nose: one for breathing, one for smelling. The smelling room is full of soft tissue that traps scent, so the smell stays after the dog breathes out. That is why a dog can follow a trail hours later — and tell which way a person walked, sniffing left, then right.

Scientists conducted an experiment: they trained dogs to smell sickness in people's breath — and the dogs could do it! Dogs can smell things at one part in a trillion. Imagine one drop in twenty swimming pools — that is how tiny a smell a dog can catch. When your dog wrinkles its forehead and sniffs the air, it is reading a whole world you cannot see.

男生: Today's idiom is "off the hook." It means you are free from a job or a trouble — you do not have to do it anymore. For example: Gaia worked for seven years, and now she is off the hook — no more rescues, naps and games! In your life: you thought you had to clean your room, but Mum says, "You are off the hook today!" Try it this week: tell your family about a time you were off the hook!
