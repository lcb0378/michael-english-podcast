女生: 先抬头看天！在中国四川都江堰，三万多架无人机同时飞上夜空，创下世界纪录。它们是怎么做到的？竖起耳朵听好啦。

男生: Raise your eyes to the night sky over Dujiangyan in Sichuan, China. At the approach of night, 33,615 drones rise together into the dark and set three Guinness World Records at once, the largest number of drones ever flown in one show!

Each drone is small, about the size of a sparrow, and carries one bright LED light. One computer can conduct the whole show, like a conductor leading an orchestra, and every drone holds its own altitude while every aspect is planned in advance.

Watch what they paint: a giant panda munching bamboo, floating islands, and the 28 constellations of the East, star pictures from old Chinese stories. The lights pulse on and off, and the drones even write a sentence across the sky! Together, they form a floating screen, the largest mid-air LED screen ever made.

The drones act as one giant paintbrush, quietly: no smoke to make you cough, no loud bangs, just light dancing in the dark.

女生: 从人造的星空，我们飞进夏夜的草丛，找一找大自然自己的小灯笼——萤火虫。它的光为什么一点都不烫？快听！

男生: Now let's meet nature's own little lamp: the firefly. A firefly is not a fly at all. It is a beetle, and it carries its light under its belly, in a special tissue, a thin layer of tiny cells.

Here is the secret: inside those cells, two chemicals meet. One is called luciferin, and the other is an enzyme called luciferase. Add oxygen from the air, and the mixture gives off light. Scientists call this bioluminescence, living light.

And here is the magic part: almost all the energy turns into light, and almost none turns into heat. A normal light bulb wastes most of its energy as heat, but a firefly's light is cold light. You can hold a firefly in your hand, and its glow will not burn you at all!

Why do they glow? Mostly to talk. Each kind of firefly flashes its own code, a pattern of pulses, like a secret language. Males flash, females answer. Some mother fireflies, pregnant with tiny eggs, keep flashing to warn hungry birds: do not eat me, I taste terrible! Each egg she lays is tinier than a grain of rice. You might think the light sits on its forehead. No! It sits under its belly.

男生: Today's idiom is "burn the midnight oil." It means to work or study late into the night. Long ago, people read by oil lamps, so working after midnight really did burn oil!
