女生: 欢迎来到 Michael 的早听晚读！我是 Cherry。今天有两位动物主角：一位是会拿画笔的大象，另一位是夜里会飞的哺乳动物——蝙蝠。准备好了吗？出发！

男生: Hello, young explorers, and welcome back to Michael's Morning Listening! Today we meet two amazing animals: an elephant who paints pictures with her trunk, and a bat — the only mammal that can truly fly. Ready? Let's go!

女生: 先说大象。在泰国清迈山区的一座大象营地里，有几头大象会画画。它们用鼻子尖卷起画笔，画出花朵和大树。想知道它们是怎么做到的吗？仔细听！

男生: In Thailand, in a camp in the hills near Chiang Mai, some elephants learned to paint. An elephant holds a brush with the tip of her trunk, moved by strong facial muscles, and sweeps colors across the paper. One painted a bunch of flowers, another painted a tree, and the crowd approved with loud cheers. Little by little, their paintings accumulated on the walls, and visitors loved them.

The most famous work was a giant painting made by eight elephants together. It was twelve meters long — longer than a school bus! It showed the green hills and soft mist of their home, and it won a place in a book of world records. The elephants did not choose the pictures all by themselves; their trainers stood beside them and helped. Still, the paintings show the special bond between elephants and people, and how clever and gentle these giants can be.

女生: 从大象的画室，我们飞进夜空。那里有一种动物，长着翅膀却不是鸟——蝙蝠。它是怎么在漆黑的夜里飞来飞去还不撞墙的？快听！

男生: Meanwhile, high in the night sky, bats are waking up late, when the sun goes down. Bats are the only mammals that can truly fly. Flying squirrels can only glide, but a bat flaps its wings and stays in the air as long as it likes. Its wings are really hands: long fingers joined by thin skin, like a hand with a kite stretched between the fingers.

But how does a bat fly in the dark without crashing? It uses echolocation — it calls out sounds so high that we cannot hear them, and listens for the echoes. The echoes tell it where things are: a tree, a wall, or a tasty moth. Statistics show that about one in every five mammal species in the world is a bat, and a single small bat can eat thousands of insects in one night. That is good news for farmers, and for our health, too, because fewer insects means fewer bites. Bats are not blind at all; they simply hear their way through the night. Some bats even sleep upside down, hanging by their feet all day long.

女生: 学知识时间到！今天的俚语和说漏嘴有关：let the cat out of the bag。字面意思是“把猫从袋子里放出来”，说的是不小心泄露了秘密。听听男主播的讲解和例句。

男生: Here's today's idiom: 'let the cat out of the bag.' When you let the cat out of the bag, you tell a secret by mistake! Here's an example: 'Dad was cooking bacon on Sunday morning when he almost let the cat out of the bag about Mum's surprise party!' A secret is like a cat in a bag — once it leaks out, you cannot put it back. So, have you ever let the cat out of the bag?

女生: 今天的故事都听完啦？我们认识了会画画的大象，又见识了夜里会飞的蝙蝠。现在轮到你们动脑筋啦，听听男主播的问题，想好了可以说给爸爸妈妈听。

男生: Let's think about our stories: elephants who paint, and bats who fly by sound. First: if you could paint with an elephant, what would you ask her to paint? Second: if you had echolocation for one night, what would you do first — catch moths, or explore a dark cave? And one last question: bats are the bosses of the night sky, so which animal would you call the boss of the day? Talk about it with your family!

女生: 好啦，今天的早听晚读就到这里。愿你像大象一样有创意，像蝙蝠一样勇敢探索黑夜。我们明天早上见！

男生: That's all for today, young explorers. Be creative like an elephant, brave like a bat — and never let the cat out of the bag! See you tomorrow!
