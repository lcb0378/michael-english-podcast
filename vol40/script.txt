男生: Now, from a warm library in Malaysia, let's travel far north, to the frozen Arctic. There lives the polar bear, the great white bear of the ice. But here is a secret: the polar bear is not white at all!

Look closely at one hair of its coat. It is not white. It is transparent, like a tiny glass tube, and it is hollow inside. The hair has no white color in it at all. So why does the bear look white? Because sunlight bounces around inside all those tiny clear tubes, and the light that comes back to our eyes looks white. It is a trick of the light!

Under all that clear hair, the bear's skin is black. Black skin is good at soaking up the sun's warmth. And under the skin there is a thick layer of fat. Together, the clear coat, the black skin, and the fat work like a warm winter jacket: even in freezing wind, very little warmth leaks out.

The white look is also perfect camouflage. On the ice, a white bear is almost invisible, so seals do not see it coming. But the bear's real colors are a surprise: clear hair, black skin, and a clever design that keeps it alive in one of the coldest places on Earth.
