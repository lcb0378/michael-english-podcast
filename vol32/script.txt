男生: Hello, young explorers, and welcome back to Michael's Morning Listening! Today we meet two amazing animals: a cat who ran a train station, and a bird that never forgets a face. One wears a tiny hat. The other keeps a list of friends — and enemies — in its head. Ready? Let's go!

女生: 先去日本乡间的一座小车站。那里的火车线快要倒闭了，车站连工作人员都撤走了。可一只花猫却留了下来，还当上了站长！它到底是怎么救活一条铁路线的？竖起耳朵听好啦。

男生: In the Japanese countryside, there is a small train station called Kishi Station. The railway company was losing money, so it cut costs and took away all the station workers. The little station was almost empty.
But the station already had a very special resident: a calico cat named Tama. Tama lived in the station shop. The railway president had a wonderful idea: make Tama the stationmaster!
So Tama got a delicate little hat and a shiny gold name tag. Her salary was a whole year of cat food! Her job was simple: greet every passenger with a smile. People laugh with delight when they meet her. Workers were instructed to build Tama her own little office.
And guess what? People came from all over Japan to meet her. The little line recovered. A study said Tama brought more than a billion yen to the local area. One small cat made a big difference to a whole town. That is one awesome cat!
Tama is almost never absent from her post. Every passenger still needs a valid ticket, even to meet the stationmaster!
Later, the station was rebuilt in the shape of a cat's face. When Tama passed away, the town mourned her, and another calico cat, Nitama, took over.

女生: 从日本飞到美国，去见一种脑子像侦探一样的鸟：乌鸦。科学家做了一个戴面具的实验，结果让人大吃一惊——乌鸦的记性到底有多好？它只记仇吗？听听就知道。

男生: Now, from Japan we fly to America, to meet a bird with a brain like a detective: the crow.
Scientists at a university wanted to test how good a crow's memory is. They wore a strange caveman mask, caught a few crows, and let them go again. The crows were not hurt, but they remembered that mask.
For years after that, crows scolded anyone who wore that mask — even young crows that were never caught. Their parents had taught them: that face means danger! The experiment exposed a surprising truth: crows can remember a single human face for years, and they teach their children, too.
But crows do not only remember bad things. In Seattle, a young girl named Gabi fed the crows in her garden. The crows remembered her kindness — and they started to bring her gifts! Little beads, buttons, a paper clip, and even a pearl-colored heart. Anything shiny that could fit in a beak became a present.
So remember: crows keep score. Be kind, and they will remember that, too.

男生: Here's today's idiom: 'curiosity killed the cat.' It means being too curious can get you into trouble! Imagine a kitten who just has to know what is in a box — and falls in headfirst! Here's an example: 'Milo wanted to peek inside the beehive, but his mother said: careful — curiosity killed the cat!' So stay curious, young explorers — but stay safe, too!
