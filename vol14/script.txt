女生: 欢迎来到 Michael 的早听晚读！我是 Cherry。今天是我们的第十四期！今天我们去海边玩：第一位主角是一只会冲浪的狗狗，它站在冲浪板上乘风破浪，可神气了；第二位主角是一群海獭，它们睡觉的时候还要手牵着手。准备好了吗？出发！

男生: Hello, young explorers, and welcome back to Michael's Morning Listening! This is our fourteenth show together! Today we are going to the beach — and the surfers there are not people. They are dogs on surfboards! Later, we meet an animal that holds hands while it sleeps. Ready? Let's dive in!

女生: 先来到美国加利福尼亚的一片阳光海滩。这里正在举行一场狗狗冲浪锦标赛！选手们要在十二分钟里冲上自己最好的海浪，评委还要看谁冲得最远、站得最稳。谁会是冠军呢？听好啦。

男生: On a sunny beach in Huntington Beach, California, crowds are cheering at the ocean. Why? A dog is surfing! This is the Surf City Surf Dog competition, where dogs ride real waves. Big dogs, small dogs, long dogs, round dogs — all standing on surfboards, ears flying! The rules are simple. Each dog gets twelve minutes to catch its best five waves. Judges watch: How long is the ride? How brave is the dog? A good surf dog rides the wave all the way to the sand, and walks off like a superstar. Every dog wears a life jacket, so everybody stays safe. The biggest superstar is a dog named Sugar. Sugar was a rescue dog, found on the street. But on a surfboard, she is pure gold: she has won this competition five times! Her paw prints are in the concrete of the Surfers' Hall of Fame — the first dog to receive this honour. And it is not only about winning. Some dogs surf in pairs, two friends on one board; some surf with their humans. The party raises money to help dogs find new homes. Sun, waves and happy dogs: what a day!

女生: 从热闹的海滩，我们漂到安静的海面上。第二位主角海獭，一辈子几乎都生活在海里，吃在海里，玩在海里，连睡觉都漂在水面上。可是，睡着了不会被海浪冲走吗？它们自有聪明的办法。

男生: Now let's float away to the quiet water where sea otters live. Sea otters spend their whole lives in the ocean. They eat, play — and yes, sleep — in the water, floating on their backs like little boats. But if you fall asleep on the sea, the waves will carry you away! Sea otters have two clever tricks. Trick one: hold hands. Otters sleep in groups called rafts, paw in paw, so nobody drifts away. One raft can hold dozens of otters, all hand in hand. Trick two is even smarter. Near the shore grow forests of kelp, rooted in the sea floor. An otter wraps kelp around its body like a rope; the kelp is an anchor, holding it in one place. And the babies? A newborn pup cannot swim or dive, so it rides on its mother's belly while she floats on her back. She grooms its fur — the thickest coat of any animal on Earth — to keep it warm. When Mother dives for food, she wraps her baby in kelp first — like parking a tiny boat. And thanks to conservation, otters are coming back to these waters.

女生: 学知识时间到！今天的俚语，和两个故事都特别搭：in the same boat。字面意思是“在同一条船上”。想想看：一条小船遇上风浪，船上的人会怎么样呢？听听男主播的讲解和例句。

男生: Here's today's idiom: "in the same boat." You and your friends are in one small boat, and the waves grow bigger. You are safe together — or wet together! Nobody can climb out alone. It means you share one problem and help each other. Like surf dogs and humans: if a wave wins, everybody gets wet! Example: "When rain stopped our picnic, we were all in the same boat, so we played games inside."

女生: 今天的故事都听完啦？我们先看了狗狗冲浪，又看了海獭牵手睡觉。现在轮到你们动脑筋啦，听听男主播的问题，想好了可以说给爸爸妈妈听。

男生: Let's think about our stories. Dogs rode real waves; otters held hands in their sleep. Now, two questions for you. First: if you could teach a dog one sport, which sport would you choose? Second: otters hold hands so they never drift apart. On a big adventure, whose hand would you hold? Talk about it with your family!

女生: 好啦，今天的早听晚读就到这里。愿你像冲浪狗狗一样勇敢，也像海獭一样，懂得拉紧朋友的手。我们明天早上见！

男生: That's all for today, young explorers. On the waves or on the water, good friends stick together — in the same boat. See you tomorrow morning!
