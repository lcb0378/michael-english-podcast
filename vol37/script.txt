女生: 欢迎来到 Michael 的早听晚读！我是 Cherry。今天我们要去沙漠里探险：先去一座沙漠中的滑雪场，在真正的雪上滑行；再潜入滚烫的泉水，认识一种不怕热的小鱼。准备好了吗？出发！

男生: Hello, young explorers, and welcome back to Michael's Morning Listening! Today we visit two wonders of the desert. First, a ski resort in the middle of hot sand, with real snow! Then, a tiny fish that swims in water almost too hot to touch. Ready? Let's go!

女生: 先说滑雪场。它不在雪山上，而是在一座沙漠城市的大商场里。外面是滚滚热浪，里面却是冬天！这是怎么做到的？竖起耳朵听好啦。

男生: In Dubai, a city in the middle of the desert, there is a ski resort inside a huge shopping mall. Outside, the sun bakes the sand. Inside, it is winter all year round! The temperature stays around minus two degrees, and real snow covers the slopes.

Here are some amazing statistics: there are five slopes, and the longest run is four hundred metres long. Every night, snow machines make fresh snow, and it accumulates into a soft white mountain. Thick walls and double doors make sure the cold air does not leak out. Meanwhile, visitors put on warm jackets and boots — the resort lends them all for free!

Families come here to ski, snowboard, and build snowmen. Children race down on sleds, and friends have snowball fights. If you stay late, the snow park glows with coloured lights. And here is the biggest surprise: visitors also get the chance to meet real penguins! King penguins and gentoo penguins waddle around their icy home, and they seem to approve of the cold. Skiing together in the desert is a wonderful way for families to bond.

女生: 从雪场出来，我们来到另一片沙漠。这里没有雪，只有一汪汪热气腾腾的泉水。里面居然住着小鱼！它们怎么能在热水里活下来？快听！

男生: Now we travel to Death Valley in America, one of the hottest places on Earth. Among the rocks and sand, there are small pools of warm spring water. And in that water lives a tiny hero: the desert pupfish. It is only about four centimetres long — smaller than your thumb!

This little fish is a champion of survival. In summer, its pool can heat up to forty-seven degrees — almost too hot to touch! In winter, the same water can drop to zero. And the water is four times saltier than the ocean. Most fish would not last a day here. The males turn bright blue in the mating season.

How does it do it? Special cells in its gills and kidneys work like tiny filters, keeping the salt balance just right. When the water gets low in oxygen, the pupfish rises to the surface, where the air meets the water. Rangers check the health of the springs every week, because these fish live nowhere else on Earth. One more pupfish lives in a single desert-cave pool, where the water stays around thirty-four degrees all year. Only a few hundred are left.

女生: 学知识时间到！今天的俚语，和身体有点不舒服有关：under the weather。字面意思是“在天气之下”，说的是感觉有点不舒服，但不是大病。听听男主播的讲解和例句。

男生: Here's today's idiom: 'under the weather.' When you feel under the weather, you feel a little sick — not seriously ill, just not quite yourself! Here's an example: 'After skiing all day in the cold, Tom felt under the weather, so he stayed home with hot soup and a good book.' So next time your nose is runny or your head feels heavy, you can say: 'I'm feeling a bit under the weather today.'

男生: Let's think about our stories: a ski resort in the desert, and a tiny fish in hot water. Second: the pupfish survives the hottest water by changing how its body works. What helps you feel better on a very hot day? Talk about it with your family!
