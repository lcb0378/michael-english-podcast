女生: 欢迎来到 Michael 的早听晚读！我是 Cherry。今天我们要认识两位了不起的“倾听者”：一位独自驾船环游世界的少女，和一种用脚来“听”远方朋友说话的大动物。一个教我们勇气，一个教我们倾听的艺术。准备好了吗？出发！

男生: Hello, young explorers, and welcome back to Michael's Morning Listening! Today we meet two great listeners: a teenage girl who sailed around the world all by herself, and the biggest land animal on Earth — an animal that listens with its feet! One teaches us courage; the other, the art of listening. Let's go!

女生: 先来讲少女水手的故事。她出生在船上，从小就会开船。十四岁那年，她向爸爸妈妈说出了一个大梦想：一个人驾船环游世界。大人们都担心，可她没有放弃。她是怎么准备的，又在海上遇到了什么？竖起耳朵听好啦。

男生: Laura Dekker was born on a boat, and she learned to sail as a small child. At fourteen, she told her parents her big dream: to sail around the world all alone. The grown-ups worried: the sea is huge, and storms do not care how old you are. But Laura did not give up. She learned to read maps and fix engines, and waited with patience.

At last she set off in her vivid red boat, a twelve-metre ketch called Guppy. She steered through the Panama Canal, across the wide Pacific, and past sunny islands. Sea spray flew over the bow, and at night she slept in short naps, one ear open for the wind. Once, in the Indian Ocean, a great storm shook her boat for days, but Laura held on and sailed on.

After five hundred and eighteen days at sea, Laura sailed into Sint Maarten. Pedestrians on the shore stopped and cheered, and the whole harbour erupted in cheers. At sixteen, she was the youngest person ever to sail around the world alone. The caption under her photo says simply: a girl, a boat, and a dream. Today she is an ambassador for young sailors, teaching them to sail and to believe in themselves.

女生: 从大海上，我们走到非洲的干旱大地上。那里住着陆地上最大的动物——大象。它的叫声低得我们几乎听不见，可远方的同伴却听得清清楚楚。它到底是怎么做到的？答案可能让你大吃一惊：它是用脚在“听”！

男生: Now let's walk onto the land of Africa, where the biggest land animal on Earth is talking in a voice we can barely hear. An elephant's rumble is so low that it sits below what our ears can catch. To us, the elephant seems almost silent, and we are nearly deaf to its deepest calls. But other elephants, kilometres away, hear it clearly. Their message comes in more than one format: sound through the air, and trembles through the ground.

How do they feel the tremble? Under an elephant's foot there is a soft pad, full of tiny sensors that pick up vibrations. The tremble travels up the bones of the leg and into the inner ear, where the brain reads it as sound. Scientists in Namibia watched elephants at a waterhole: when a faraway rumble shook the ground, they froze, lifted one foot, and turned towards the sound. They even show fine discrimination — they can tell a family member's rumble from a stranger's. So an elephant does not only listen with its ears. It listens with its feet, too.

女生: 学知识时间到！今天的俚语，和一路相伴、不离不弃有关：through thick and thin。字面意思是“穿过密林和稀疏的小路”，说的是无论顺境还是逆境都在一起。听听男主播的讲解和例句。

男生: Here's today's idiom: 'through thick and thin.' It means staying with someone in good times and in bad times — through thick forests and thin paths. Laura and her little boat went through thick and thin together: sunny days and great storms. Here's an example: 'True friends stay together through thick and thin.' Who has stayed with you through thick and thin?

女生: 今天的故事都听完啦？我们认识了独自环游世界的少女劳拉，又知道了大象用脚听远方的朋友说话。现在轮到你们动脑筋啦，听听男主播的问题，想好了可以说给爸爸妈妈听。

男生: Let's think about our stories. Laura sailed alone for more than five hundred days, and an elephant can hear a friend kilometres away — through the ground! Two questions for you. First: what dream would you chase, even if grown-ups said it was too hard? Second: if you could talk through the ground like an elephant, what would you say? Talk about it with your family!

女生: 好啦，今天的早听晚读就到这里。愿你像劳拉一样勇敢，像大象一样善于倾听。我们明天早上见！

男生: That's all for today, young explorers. Be brave like Laura, and listen like an elephant. And remember: true friends stay together through thick and thin! See you tomorrow morning!
