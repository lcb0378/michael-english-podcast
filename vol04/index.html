🔬 Part 4：长难句深度拆解与练习

经典原句：
Sgiath is now believed to be the first dog in the world trained to smell a dangerous sea creature — the carpet sea squirt, also known as "sea vomit".

保姆级结构拆解：
  • is believed to be 是被动语态，表示"被认为是……"。科普英语里介绍新发现时，常用被动语态代替 people believe，显得更客观、更地道。
  • trained to smell a dangerous sea creature 是过去分词短语作后置定语，修饰 the first dog，相当于 who was trained to smell...。注意 the first（序数词）后面常接不定式或过去分词短语，这个用法在英语里非常常见。
  • 破折号后的 the carpet sea squirt 是同位语，点出这种危险生物到底是谁；also known as "sea vomit" 是过去分词短语作补充说明，告诉你它的外号。
  • 整句逻辑链：身份认定（被认为是第一只）→ 限定条件（受过嗅探训练）→ 任务（找出危险生物）→ 点名（地毯海鞘）→ 外号（"海呕吐物"）。一句话把新闻主角介绍得清清楚楚，是新闻英语"介绍主角"的万能句型。
句型练习（练的就是上面拆解的三个知识点）

A. 被动句型：用 is / are believed to be 改写
  1. People believe Sgiath is the best sniffer dog in Britain.
    → Sgiath ____________________ the best sniffer dog in Britain.
  2. People believe the two moons of Mars are pieces of a broken planet.
    → The two moons of Mars ____________________ pieces of a broken planet.
B. 过去分词后置定语：用括号里的短语补全
  1. Sgiath is the first dog in the world ____________________ a dangerous sea creature. (trained to smell)
  2. The capsule is the first box ____________________ pieces of a moon back to Earth. (built to carry)
C. 同位语：补全句子
  1. Phobos, ____________________, is about twenty-two kilometers wide.（火星两颗卫星中较大的那颗）
  2. The carpet sea squirt, also known as "____________________", came from abroad.
D. 翻译练习：把这两句译成中文（不提供译文，自己试着写）
  1. The little robot is believed to be the first machine in the world built to clean the deep sea — a quiet helper, also known as "the Sea Sweeper".
  2. My grandpa is believed to be the first man in our village trained to fix old radios — a kind man, also known as "Doctor Radio".
