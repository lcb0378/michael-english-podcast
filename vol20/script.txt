女生: 先去泰国的一家动物园。那里住着一只小小的倭河马，它的名字在泰语里的意思是“弹跳的猪肉”，听起来就很好笑。它的饲养员把它的日常拍成小视频发到网上，没想到，一夜之间，全世界都爱上了它。

男生: Say hello to Moo Deng, a pygmy hippo at Khao Kheow Open Zoo in Thailand. She is much smaller than her big cousins, and rounder than a beach ball. Her name means "bouncy pork" in Thai, and fans chose it in a vote. She comes from a pork dynasty: Mother Jona, Father Tony, and all her brothers and sisters carry porky names. Her keeper, Atthapon, began posting videos of her on social media — splashing in her pool, opening her mouth wide, taking awkward little steps. The videos went viral worldwide. Soon everyone seemed astonished: how could one small hippo hold so much appeal? When she yawned or bounced across her pen, crowds erupted with laughter. Fans travelled far to see her, and zoo visitor numbers jumped to three times their old size. The zoo set up a live camera service, so fans on far continents could watch her splash and sleep. She got her own song, appeared in a comedy show, and even picked the winner of an election by choosing between two cakes! Best of all, her fame helps every pygmy hippo, an endangered species that needs protection.

女生: 从热带的动物园，我们一路向北，飞到寒冷的阿拉斯加。第二位主角是一只小小的木蛙。别的青蛙过冬要么挖洞，要么逃走，它却选了一条最不可思议的路：把自己冻起来。它的心脏会停跳好几个月，可春天一到，它又蹦蹦跳跳地活过来啦。

男生: Now meet the wood frog of Alaska, the northernmost frog in the world. Most frogs hibernate deep under the ground. This one does not. When the cold comes, it climbs under dead leaves — and lets itself freeze. Ice grows inside its body, between its skin and muscles, and even inside its eyes. Soon almost seventy percent of the water in its body is ice. Its blood freezes, its breathing stops, and its heart stops beating. To any eye, the frog looks quite dead. But the frog has a sweet secret. Before the freeze, its liver fills its cells with glucose — a sugar that works like antifreeze. The sugar protects each cell from damage, so no ice can grow inside them. Frozen solid for months, the wood frog does not breathe or move, yet when spring warms the pond, its heart starts again, as if nothing had happened. And this trick has real significance for us. Scientists hope it may one day help doctors keep donated organs alive for longer. All that from one small frog, sleeping under the leaves!

Listen to this: Max hated his haircut — the barber shaved it too short, and he hid under a hat. But at swimming class, with almost no hair, Max swam faster than ever — and won his race! The terrible haircut was a blessing in disguise.
