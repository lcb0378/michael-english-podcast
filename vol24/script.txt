男生: Hello, young explorers, and welcome back to Michael's Morning Listening! This is our twenty-fourth show! Today we meet a penguin that proposes with a pebble, and a koala whose fingerprints look exactly like yours. A builder from Antarctica, and a fingerprint star from the treetops. Let's go!

女生: 先去寒冷的南极，认识一群穿着黑白"礼服"的企鹅。企鹅不会说话，却会用小石头表达心意。它们是怎么"求婚"的呢？

男生: Our first hero lives in Antarctica, the coldest place on Earth. It is the Adélie penguin, a bird in a black-and-white garment that looks like a suit. In spring, the males arrive first. They do not bring flowers. They bring pebbles! A male picks up a smooth stone in his beak and walks to a female, with courage. If she likes his stone, she may place it in her nest. It looks like a proposal! Scientists watch from a boat, its engine humming softly.

But the pebble is not just a pretty gift. It is the primary building material of the nest. The pair builds a round nest of stones, and the nest keeps the eggs safe and dry. When snow melts, the water runs between the stones instead of flooding the nest. Sharp ice could wound a chick's soft feet, so a dry nest matters. So the male is really saying, "Look, I can build a safe home!" Sometimes a male steals a stone from a neighbour's nest, and a fight erupts!

To everyone's delight, the pair works together, taking turns on the eggs and fetching more stones. Their footprints fade in the snow, but their little stone nest stays. A penguin's proposal is not about a diamond ring. It is about teamwork and a safe place for babies to grow.

女生: 从冰天雪地飞到澳大利亚的桉树林！第二位主角整天抱着树叶大嚼特嚼，看起来懒洋洋的。可是它的手指上，藏着一个让科学家都惊讶的秘密。这是什么秘密呢？

男生: Our second hero is the koala, a grey furry animal from Australia. It sleeps most of the day in a eucalyptus tree, holding on tight. By coincidence, a scientist who was holding a koala looked closely at its fingers — and got a big surprise. Under the soft flesh of its fingertips were tiny ridges, loops, and whorls. They looked just like human fingerprints!

The scientist studied the prints under a microscope, and the surprise grew bigger. Even experts could hardly tell a koala's print from a human's! How can this be? Humans and koalas are not close cousins at all. Yet both grew the same kind of skin on their fingers. Scientists call this convergent evolution: two different animals solving the same problem in the same way.

For the koala, the grip is everything. Its ridges help it hold smooth branches and pick soft leaves, like your fingerprints help you hold a pencil. So when you press your finger on a window, imagine a koala doing the same in a tree. Two hands, two worlds — and almost the same print!

女生: 学知识时间到！今天的俚语是 like two peas in a pod，字面意思是"像豌豆荚里的两颗豌豆"——用来形容两个人长得像、形影不离，就像双胞胎一样。听听男主播的小故事。

男生: Here's today's idiom: "like two peas in a pod." Peas in one pod look the same! So when two people are always together, we say they are like two peas in a pod. Listen: Pip and Pop are two penguin chicks. They waddle together, nap together, and lose their feathers on the same day. "You two are like two peas in a pod!" laughs their mother.

男生: Let's think about our stories. A penguin builds a nest stone by stone, and a koala's fingers carry prints like ours. Two questions. First: what gift would you bring to ask a friend to build with you? Second: what do your fingerprints help you do? Talk about it with your family!
