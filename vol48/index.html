🔬 Part 4：长难句深度拆解与练习

经典原句：
Scientists conducted an experiment: they turned some flowers to face west, and the east-facing flowers attracted five times as many pollinators!

保姆级结构拆解：
  • conducted an experiment 是固定搭配：conduct（进行）+ an experiment（一个实验），意思是“做了一个实验”。conduct 后面常常跟 experiment、research（研究）、survey（调查）这类词。
  • turned some flowers to face west 是turn + 名词 + to face：turn（转动、转朝）+ some flowers（一些花儿）+ to face west（朝向西边），意思是“把一些花儿转得朝向西边”。to face 在这里表示转动后的方向。
  • five times as many pollinators 是倍数表达：five times（五倍）+ as many（和……一样多）+ pollinators（授粉昆虫，可数名词复数），意思是“五倍数量的授粉昆虫”。说“是另一方的五倍”时，英语就用“数字 + times as many + 复数名词”。
  • 整句逻辑链：做实验（conducted an experiment）→ 转花朝西（turned some flowers to face west）→ 数结果（five times as many pollinators）。一句话里藏着固定搭配、方向结构和倍数表达三个机关。

句型练习 · 互动版（练的就是上面拆解的三个知识点；点选项作答，全部选完后点「核对答案」）

A. conduct + experiment 固定搭配：用括号里的词补全

Scientists ____ an experiment to test the flowers.

正确答案：conducted

Scientists ____ experiments every year.

正确答案：conduct

B. 数字 + times as many + 复数名词：用括号里的词补全

East-facing flowers attracted five times as ____ pollinators as west-facing ones.

正确答案：many

The giant flower attracted three times as ____ visitors as a small one.

正确答案：many

C. turn + 名词 + to face：用括号里的词补全

Scientists turned some flowers ____ face west.

正确答案：to

Lucy turned her flower ____ face the sun.

正确答案：to
