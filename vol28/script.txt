男生: Hello, young explorers, and welcome back to Michael's Morning Listening! Today we fly over a forest with a buzzing robot, then dig underground to find a secret internet made by mushrooms. Our first hero has no hands — but it can plant ten thousand trees in a single day. Our second hero never speaks a word — yet it helps whole forests share food. Let's go!

女生: 先戴好头盔，坐上小飞机。加拿大的森林里，有一群会飞的“种树工人”。它们不用铲子、不用水桶，种子像雨点一样从天上落下来。竖起耳朵听好啦。

男生: In Canada, a company called Flash Forest sends drones to plant trees. First, small mapping drones fly over land where fires or cutting have left the ground bare, and find the best places for new trees. Then the planting drones take off, carrying tiny seed pods. Each pod holds a seed that has already started to grow, wrapped in food and soil. The drone fires the pods into the ground, one every second. One drone can plant more than ten thousand trees in a day. By hand, a worker plants about one thousand five hundred trees a day. After planting, other drones spray water and food over the young trees and watch from the sky to see which ones grow well. It takes patience — a seed needs years to become a tall tree. Soon, vivid green shoots rise from the ground. Every forest starts with one small seed. And now, the seeds can fly.

女生: 从天上回到地面，蹲下来，拨开落叶。森林地下藏着一张看不见的大网——不是电话线，不是蜘蛛网，而是一张用蘑菇丝织成的网。它到底怎么帮树木分享食物？仔细听。

男生: Under every forest floor hides a secret network. It is made by fungi — the same family as mushrooms. Thin white threads, called hyphae, spread through the soil and wrap around tree roots. The fungi give the trees water and minerals from the soil. In return, the trees give the fungi sugars they make from sunlight. Scientists call this the Wood Wide Web. Big old mother trees sit at the centre of the web. They send extra food to young trees growing in the shade. In one famous test, birch trees shared food with fir trees when the firs needed it most, and the firs shared back in another season. When insects attack one tree, its neighbours can notice the warning and make their leaves taste bitter. After rain, mushrooms erupt from the ground — just the fruits of the fungi, like apples on a tree. The real body of the fungus lives at the bottom of the soil, quietly feeding the forest. So trees are not lonely fighters — under the ground, they share, warn, and grow together.

女生: 学知识时间到！今天的俚语，和“找错地方”有关：barking up the wrong tree。字面意思是“对着错的树狂吠”，说的是找错了对象、怪错了人。听听男主播的讲解和例句。

男生: Here's today's idiom: "barking up the wrong tree." Imagine a dog barking at a tree — but the squirrel hides in a different tree! If you are barking up the wrong tree, you are looking in the wrong place or blaming the wrong person. For a long time, people thought trees only fought each other for sunlight. They were barking up the wrong tree — under the ground, trees share and help each other. Here's an example: "When Tom lost his keys, he blamed his sister — but he was barking up the wrong tree. The keys were in his own pocket!" So, next time something goes wrong, check: are you barking up the right tree?

女生: 今天的故事都听完啦？我们先是跟着无人机种下了一万颗种子，又钻进地下，看见了蘑菇织的大网。现在轮到你们动脑筋啦，听听男主播的问题，想好了可以说给爸爸妈妈听。

男生: Let's think about our stories: flying robots that plant trees, and a hidden web of fungi that feeds a forest. Now, two questions for you. Talk about it with your family!
