男生: Now, let's dive under the waves and meet the seahorse, a small fish with a horse-like head and a tail that curls like a spring. Seahorses are slow swimmers with a big secret: in seahorse families, it is the father who bears the babies!

Here is how it works. First, let me clarify: the father does not lay the eggs. The mother puts her eggs into a special pouch on the father's belly. The father keeps the eggs safe inside the pouch, just like a warm pocket. He feeds them, gives them clean water, and protects them from danger. After two to four weeks, the babies are ready. The father pushes, and in total, hundreds of little seahorses, each nearly transparent and no bigger than your fingernail, swim out into the sea!

But here is the sad part: baby seahorses must look after themselves from the very first minute — their parents do not feed or teach them. They hide behind a curtain of green sea grass hoping no bigger fish finds them. One scientist remarked that seahorse fathers are among the best dads in the animal world, because they give their babies the safest start in life.
