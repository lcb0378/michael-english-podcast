男生: Hello, young explorers, and welcome back to Michael's Morning Listening! This is our twenty-second show together! Today we climb the tallest mountain on Earth with a man who cannot see it, and we dive under the sea to meet an animal with three hearts! One story is about courage. One story is about a body full of surprises. Let's go!

女生: 先去世界最高峰。有一位登山家，他从小就看不见东西，可他偏偏爱上了登山。他靠双手摸索岩石、听朋友的声音，一步一步往上爬。最后，他竟然站上了珠穆朗玛峰的峰顶！

男生: Erik Weihenmayer was born with weak eyes, and his eyesight faded slowly until, as a young boy, he could not see at all. But losing his sight did not stop him. He learned to climb rocks by touch, feeling every crack with his fingers, and listening to the voices of his friends. Step by step, his courage grew. He wore thick garments against the cold wind, and he kept going even when his hands got small wounds from ice and stone. Then came his greatest climb: Mount Everest, the tallest mountain in the world. Guided by his friends' voices and bells, Erik reached the top — the first blind person ever to do it, to the delight of his whole team, who erupted with cheers. He did not stop there. He went on to climb the highest peak on every continent, seven mountains in all. Touch was his primary guide, and courage was his engine. Erik says what is inside you is stronger than what stands in your way.

女生: 从高高的雪山，我们潜入大海。第二位主角软软的、没有骨头，却长着三颗心脏！它的血还是蓝色的呢。快来看看这颗会"停一停"的心脏是怎么回事。

男生: Now dive with me into the sea and meet the octopus, a soft animal with no bones at all. It has not one heart, but three! Two small hearts, called branchial hearts, push blood to its gills, where the blood picks up oxygen from the water. Then a third, bigger heart, the systemic heart, sends the oxygen-rich blood around the body, to every cell. And here is the strangest part: the octopus's blood is blue! Our blood is red because it uses iron, but octopus blood uses copper, and copper turns it blue. When the octopus swims fast, its big heart stops for a while. Do not worry — the octopus is not dead. It just gets too tired to keep pumping. That is why octopuses usually crawl along the sea floor instead of swimming. It is no coincidence that they are such good crawlers: eight soft arms, covered in suckers, can grip rocks and squeeze the whole body through tiny gaps. One octopus slipped out of its pen in an aquarium and escaped back to the sea! With three hearts, blue blood, and a body of soft flesh, the octopus is alive with surprises.

女生: 学知识时间到！今天的俚语是 pull someone's leg，字面意思是"拉别人的腿"——可不是真的拉腿哦，它的意思是开玩笑、逗别人。听听男主播的小故事。

男生: Here's today's idiom: "pull someone's leg." It does not mean you really pull a leg! It means you tease someone with a joke. Listen to this: Tom told his friend Sam that a shark lived in the school swimming pool. Sam put on his swimming hat and ran to look. Then Tom laughed and said, "I was only pulling your leg!" When someone pulls your leg, they are joking, not telling the truth.

女生: 今天的故事都听完啦？我们认识了勇敢的盲人登山家，又见识了三颗心脏的章鱼。现在轮到你们动脑筋啦，听听男主播的问题，想好了可以说给爸爸妈妈听。

男生: Let's think about our stories. A blind climber reached the top of the world, and an octopus carries three hearts in its soft body. Two questions for you. First: Erik climbed by touch and by listening. If you could not see, what would help you most — touch, sound, or smell? Second: if you had three hearts like an octopus, what would you do with the extra one? Talk about it with your father and mother!

女生: 好啦，今天的早听晚读就到这里。记住：勇气能带你登上任何一座高山，开玩笑也要大家一起笑才好玩哦。我们明天早上见！

男生: That's all for today, young explorers. Remember: courage can carry you up any mountain, and a joke is only fun when everyone laughs. Stay curious! See you tomorrow morning!
