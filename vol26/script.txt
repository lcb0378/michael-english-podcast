女生: 欢迎来到 Michael 的早听晚读！我是 Cherry。今天我们要认识两位了不起的“行动派”。第一位主角有四条腿，却不是小狗——它能爬山、蹚冰河，还永远不知道累。第二位主角比你的大拇指还小，却会一招全世界独一无二的本事。准备好了吗？出发！

男生: Hello, young explorers, and welcome back to Michael's Morning Listening! Today we meet two amazing movers. Our first hero has four legs, but it is not a dog. It climbs mountains, wades through icy rivers, and never gets tired. Our second hero is smaller than your thumb, yet it can do something no other bird on Earth can do. Let's go!

女生: 先去一座高山上。泰山的石阶上，游客们都停下脚步、瞪大了眼睛——因为正有一只机器狗，背着沉甸甸的包，一步一步往上爬。它到底是来干什么的？竖起耳朵听好啦。

男生: High on the stone steps of Mount Tai in China, a line of pedestrians stops and stares. Coming up the steps is a robot dog with a heavy bag on its back. These robot dogs work as mountain porters, carrying heavy loads so human porters can rest. One robot dog, called B2-W, can carry forty kilograms and walk fifty kilometres on one charge. Cheers erupt as it arrives at the top. Another robot dog, called Lynx, works high on a cold mountain, where the air is thin and it is thirty degrees below zero. It climbs slopes as steep as forty-five degrees, splashes through icy rivers eighty centimetres deep, cold spray flying around its legs, and keeps its balance. Its cameras have sharp discrimination: they can tell a safe rock from a loose one. The robot's patience never runs out: it can try the same steep path again until it finds a safe way up. They are quick, too. One of them, called HOUND, holds a world record: it ran one hundred metres in less than twenty seconds. They are ambassadors for a new kind of helper. On a mountain, it is a champion.

女生: 从冰冷的高山，我们飞到阳光明媚的花园。有一只小鸟，喝花蜜的时候不用转身，居然能直接倒着飞出来！它是怎么做到的？科学家早就研究透了它的秘密。

男生: Now meet a bird with a great trick. The hummingbird is no bigger than your thumb, and it drinks sweet nectar from flowers. Most birds must turn around to leave a flower. Not the hummingbird. It simply flies backwards, straight out of the flower. It is the only bird on Earth that can do this. The secret is in its wings. Most birds flap up and down, and only push air on the way down. A hummingbird's wings move in a figure eight — they push air on the way up and on the way down, too. Its wings can beat fifty to eighty times every second. Its shoulder is a ball-and-socket joint, like yours, so its wings can turn in every direction. A hummingbird's flight muscles make up nearly one third of its body. It must eat all day, and males even fight over the best flowers. Its throat shines like a diamond in the sun, vivid green and red. When it flies away, its soft hum fades into the garden. Evolution gave this tiny bird a wonderful gift: wings that can carry it forwards, backwards, and even upside down.

女生: 学知识时间到！今天的俚语，和登山的机器狗特别配：keep an eye on。字面意思是“留一只眼睛看着”，说的是认真看管、留意。听听男主播的讲解和例句。

男生: Here's today's idiom: "keep an eye on." It means to watch something or someone carefully. The engineers keep an eye on the robot dog as it climbs the mountain. Here's an example: "Please keep an eye on my little brother while I buy the tickets." So, keep an eye on things you care about — and keep an eye on the hummingbird, too, or it will zoom away before you blink!
