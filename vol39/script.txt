女生: 先说纸飞机。这位爷爷叫约翰·柯林斯，从小就爱折纸飞机，几十年里折了几百架，不断改进。他最得意的一架叫“苏珊号”，还请了一位橄榄球运动员朋友来投。到底飞了多远？仔细听！

男生: John Collins has loved paper planes since he was a boy. He folded hundreds of them — big, small, fast, and funny. He was not an accountant, but he counted every fold and every test flight until his planes flew straight and far. His best design was named Suzanne, after his wife. It used just one sheet of paper, with long, thin wings. But a great plane needs a great throw, so John asked his friend Joe Ayoob, a strong football player, to throw it.

One day, inside a huge empty airplane hangar, Joe threw Suzanne with all his strength. The little plane flew and flew — farther than five school buses in a row — and landed 69.14 meters away. The judges measured it carefully and approved the new world record! If a fold is loose, air leaks through the gap and the plane wobbles, so every fold must be neat and tight. And when a test plane crashed, John did not mourn it — he just folded again. That is the bond between a designer and a thrower: one dreams, one throws, and together they fly into the record books.

女生: 从天上回到地面，看看小蚂蚁。蚂蚁看起来整天跑来跑去，好像从来不睡觉。真的吗？科学家用摄像机盯着火蚁窝看，发现了一个秘密，快听！

男生: Meanwhile, down in the grass, ants look like they never sleep. They run here and there, day and night. But scientists filmed a fire ant nest, and they found a secret: ants do sleep — they just sleep in tiny pieces! A worker ant takes about 253 naps every day, and each nap lasts just over a minute. All those little naps accumulate into almost five hours of sleep.

The statistics are amazing. Because workers nap at different times, about 80 out of every 100 ants are awake and working at any moment, so the nest never stops. The queen sleeps much longer — about nine hours a day in longer naps, with her antennae twitching like she is dreaming. Ants do not have eyelids, so they cannot close their eyes. When a worker finds a crumb of bacon, she calls her sisters to help carry it home. And every morning, she washes her face with her front legs — her own quick facial! So when you see an ant rushing past, remember: she is just between naps.

男生: Here's today's idiom: 'rock the boat.' When you rock the boat, you upset a calm situation or cause trouble in a group. Here's an example: 'Our class had a happy plan for the picnic, and nobody wanted to rock the boat, so we all agreed to go to the park.' Rocking a real boat is fun for a second — and wet for everyone! So, when do you think it is good to speak up, even if it rocks the boat a little?
