女生: 欢迎来到 Michael 的早听晚读！我是 Cherry。今天是第二十一期！第一位主角，是一份在天上种出来的沙拉；第二位主角，是一只毛里长着小花园的树懒。一个在空间站里种菜，一个把花园穿在身上。准备好了吗？出发！

男生: Hello, young explorers, and welcome back to Michael's Morning Listening! This is our twenty-first show together! Today we visit a garden floating high above Earth, and an animal that wears a garden on its back. One grows dinner in space. One grows a forest in its fur. Let's go!

女生: 先飞上太空。国际空间站里没有土壤、没有花园，可宇航员们偏偏想在那里种出生菜。没有泥土怎么办？没有上下之分，植物往哪儿长？听听他们的办法。

男生: High above Earth, inside the International Space Station, astronauts decided to grow salad. But space has no soil, and no up or down. So NASA built a small garden box called Veggie. The seeds slept in little bags called plant pillows, under lamps of red and blue light. Water moved along small wicks, because in space water does not fall — it floats. Light, water and air are the primary needs of every plant, in space or on Earth. After about a month, the crew picked red romaine lettuce, a variety called "Outredgeous." They cleaned each leaf with a wipe, then tasted it. "It tastes like arugula," one astronaut said, to everyone's delight. Cheers erupted around the table. It takes courage to taste food grown where no food has ever grown. Half the leaves were saved and frozen for study back on Earth. The rest was eaten with olive oil and vinegar — the first space salad, grown high above the dinner table.

女生: 从空间站回到地球的雨林。树懒是世界上最慢的哺乳动物，它一星期才下一次树。可就是这身慢悠悠的毛，里面居然藏着一个小花园——有绿藻、有飞蛾，还有一套神奇的循环。

男生: Now meet the three-toed sloth, the slowest mammal in the world. It sleeps, eats leaves and barely moves — but look at its shaggy fur. Each hair has tiny grooves that hold rainwater. In that wet fur, green algae grows, turning the sloth's coat into a green garment. The green is perfect camouflage: among the leaves, the sloth almost disappears. But the garden has more tenants: tiny sloth moths live in the fur. Once a week, the sloth climbs slowly down to the forest floor — a long and risky trip — and the moths lay their eggs in its droppings. When the young moths grow up, they fly back into the fur. When old moths die there, their bodies break down and feed the algae, like fertilizer. It is no coincidence that sloths with more moths grow more algae. And here is the astonishing part: scientists think the sloth sometimes licks its own fur and eats the algae for extra food. In the dry season, when rain is rare, the green fades a little — but the garden waits for the next rain. A sloth is not just one animal — it is a whole small world.

女生: 学知识时间到！今天的俚语是 head in the clouds，字面意思是“脑袋在云里”——形容一个人总在发呆、想别的事情，心思不在眼前。听听男主播的讲解和小故事。

男生: Here's today's idiom: "head in the clouds." Your head is where you think, and clouds are high in the sky, far away. So if your head is in the clouds, your thoughts are far away — you are daydreaming, not paying attention. Listen to this: During science class, Mia had her head in the clouds, dreaming about growing lettuce in space. The teacher asked, "Mia, what do plants need?" Mia blinked and said, "Light, water, and... a rocket?" The whole class laughed. Mia's head had been in the clouds — but her dream was a good one!

女生: 今天的故事都听完啦？我们先去了空间站的菜园，又认识了把花园穿在身上的树懒。现在轮到你们动脑筋啦，听听男主播的问题，想好了可以说给爸爸妈妈听。

男生: Let's think about our stories. Astronauts grew salad in space, and a sloth carries a garden in its fur. Two questions for you. First: if you could grow one food in space, what would it be, and why? Second: the sloth's fur garden helps it hide and find food. If you could wear a garden, what would you grow in it? Talk about it with your family!

女生: 好啦，今天的早听晚读就到这里。记住：别总把脑袋放在云里哦，不过偶尔做做太空梦，也没什么不好。我们明天早上见！
