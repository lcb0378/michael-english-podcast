男生: Hello, young explorers, and welcome back to Michael's Morning Listening! Today's stories are about love and courage in the animal world. First, a talking parrot who became a fire hero. Then, penguins — do they stay with one partner for life? Let's find out!

女生: 先说鹦鹉小英雄。在法国，有一只鹦鹉叫 Baby，它平时晚上从来不说话，可有一天夜里，它突然又咳嗽又大喊。到底发生了什么？竖起耳朵听好啦。

男生: In a small town in France, there lives a parrot named Baby. Baby is an African grey parrot, and like many parrots, he can copy human words. But Baby does something very special at night: he stays quiet. His owners say they never hear him after bedtime.

One night, everything changed. Baby started to cough, again and again, and then he yelled, 'It's hot! It's hot!' His owners woke up at once. They knew something was wrong, because Baby never talks at night. They followed the smell through a curtain of smoke and found a fire behind their dishwasher. They turned it off quickly and called the firefighters.

The firefighters remarked that without Baby's warning, the family might have breathed in dangerous smoke in their sleep. Baby became a hero! Parrots are clever birds. Scientists have found that African grey parrots can learn many words and even understand some simple ideas. Baby did not just copy sounds — he used his words at the right moment, and that made the difference.

女生: 从鹦鹉的叫声，我们摇摇摆摆走到寒冷的南方，去看看企鹅的爱情。企鹅真的从一而终吗？答案可能和你想的不一样，快听！

男生: Now, from a talking hero, let's waddle down to the cold south, where penguins live. People often say penguins stay with one partner for life. Is that true? Let me clarify: it depends! Most penguins stay with one partner for a breeding season. They share the work: keeping the egg warm, finding food, and protecting the chick.

Some penguins are faithful. Gentoo penguins return to the same partner about nine times out of ten, and Magellanic penguins also stay together year after year. But other penguins are different. Emperor penguins usually find a new partner each year, and king penguins change partners even more often. Scientists studied twelve kinds of penguins and found that, in total, about seven out of ten pairs stay together from one year to the next.

Why do penguins change partners? Sometimes the old partner comes back too late, or the pair fails to raise a chick, so they try again with someone new. And here is a lovely detail: a male penguin often brings his partner a smooth pebble as a gift. If she accepts it, they build a nest together. So penguins may not always stay together for life, but they take their family duties very seriously — and that is something to appreciate.

男生: Here's today's idiom: 'the ball is in your court.' In a ball game, when the ball is in your court, it is your turn to play. In everyday life, it means it is your turn to act or to decide. Here's an example: 'The firefighters showed us how to stay safe. Now the ball is in your court — check the smoke alarm in your home with your parents!' So, young explorers, the ball is in your court: what will you do?
