女生: 欢迎来到 Michael 的早听晚读！我是 Cherry。今天我们要认识两位特别的“小帮手”：一位是会自己认路的送货机器人，另一位是住在海底、眼睛超级厉害的皮皮虾。准备好了吗？出发！

男生: Hello, young explorers, and welcome back to Michael's Morning Listening! Today we meet two amazing helpers: a little robot that knows every street, and a tiny sea animal with the most awesome eyes on Earth. Ready? Let's go!

女生: 先去人行道上看看。有一个白色的小盒子，长着六个轮子，自己在路上慢慢走——它可不是玩具，它是送货机器人，正要把午饭送到别人家门口呢！它是怎么认路的，又怎么躲开行人和狗狗？竖起耳朵听好啦。

男生: On a quiet sidewalk, a small white box on six wheels rolls along all by itself. It is a delivery robot, and it is carrying lunch to someone's home. It does not need a driver, because it drives itself! Cameras, radar, and GPS are its eyes and map. It moves at walking speed. When a dog runs in front of it, it stops and waits. When the road is clear, it rolls on again. If it gets lost, a human helper far away can instruct it what to do, and it recovers its route in seconds. At your door, you type in the valid code on your phone, the lid opens, and there is your lunch! These little robots move silently, and they never get tired.

Before its first trip, the robot studies a map of every street, every corner, and every crossing. It knows where to turn and where to wait. At a crossing, it looks at the traffic lights, like you do. People on the sidewalk smile at it, and children wave. Some people give it a name, like a pet! It is small, but it does an awesome job.

女生: 从人行道潜入海底！第二位主角登场了：皮皮虾。它个头不大，却有一双动物世界里最厉害的眼睛，还藏着一记快如闪电的“拳头”。它的眼睛到底能看到什么？它的拳头又有多快？

男生: Now dive under the sea and meet the mantis shrimp, a small animal with a big punch. Its eyes are the most awesome in the animal world. We have three kinds of color sensors in our eyes. The mantis shrimp has twelve to sixteen! Its eyes can see colors we cannot imagine. They can also see ultraviolet light, and a secret kind of light called polarized light. Those super eyes expose a hidden world of colors all around it. But watch out for its punch! Its club-like arms strike at twenty-three meters per second, faster than you can blink, faster than you can hear a clap. The punch is so strong that it makes tiny bubbles in the water, and when the bubbles burst, they help crack open shells. The mantis shrimp uses its delicate-looking eyes and its punch to hunt, to fight, and to guard its home.

Each eye sits on a little stalk and can move on its own, so the shrimp can look two ways at once. Scientists study these awesome eyes because they want to build better cameras and robots. The mantis shrimp is small, but its eyes are a big lesson in how nature solves hard problems.

女生: 学知识时间到！今天的俚语，和机器人看地图、我们认真学习的样子有关：hit the books。字面意思是“去打书”，其实是说埋头苦读、认真学习。听听男主播的讲解和例句。

男生: Here's today's idiom: 'hit the books.' It means to study hard, with your nose in your books! Just like the little robot studies its map before every trip, we hit the books before a big test. Here's an example: 'The big test is tomorrow, so tonight Mia will hit the books.' When do you hit the books?

女生: 今天的故事都听完啦？我们认识了自己认路的送货机器人，又见识了眼睛超级厉害的皮皮虾。现在轮到你们动脑筋啦，听听男主播的问题，想好了可以说给爸爸妈妈听。

男生: Let's think about our stories. A robot drives itself to your door, and a mantis shrimp sees colors we cannot see. Two questions for you. First: if a delivery robot came to your home, what would you ask it to bring — lunch, books, or something funny? Second: if you could see one secret color that no one else can see, what would you do with it? Talk about it with your family!
